## Résumé
In a world divided by fire and philosophy, two civilisations have mastered the art of cooking - but never spoken the same language. The story follows an Eastern chef who enters a Western culinary tournament and wins with unknown techniques, drawing the attention of both worlds.

## Thématique culinaire
Cooking is the central theme as two civilizations with different culinary traditions clash and eventually learn from each other. The culinary tournament provides a competitive framework for showcasing cooking skills and cross-cultural culinary exchange.

## Éléments fantastiques
The world is divided by 'fire and philosophy,' suggesting a mythological or fantastical division. The concept of civilizations mastering cooking as an art form adds a fantasy layer to the culinary competition setting.

## Pertinence pour le projet
High — The cross-cultural culinary tournament concept could inspire game mechanics around cooking competitions, cultural exchange, or unlocking new recipes through interacting with different factions.
