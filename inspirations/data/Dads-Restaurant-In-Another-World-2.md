## Résumé
In the Chaos City of Nolan Continent, there's a peculiar restaurant. Here, elves have to share tables with dwarves, orcs are strictly forbidden from making noise, dragons can only sit around the small square in front of the restaurant, and demons even need to bring their own special stools... Yet, despite these bizarre rules, a long line forms outside the restaurant every day.

## Thématique culinaire
Continuation of the restaurant concept from Season 1. The chef continues to serve fantasy races in a world where the rules of dining are as strange as the world itself. Cooking remains the core activity.

## Éléments fantastiques
Continuation of the fantasy world with elves, dwarves, orcs, and dragons. The addition of zombies and time travel elements expands the fantasy scope. The restaurant remains the anchor in this bizarre world.

## Pertinence pour le projet
Very High — Same concept as Season 1, expanded. The restaurant serving fantasy races with bizarre rules provides rich material for game design around restaurant management in a fantasy setting.
