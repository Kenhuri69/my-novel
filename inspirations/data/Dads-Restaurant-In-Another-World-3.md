## Résumé
In the Chaos City of Nolan Continent, a peculiar restaurant operates where elves share tables with dwarves, orcs are forbidden from making noise, dragons sit around a small square, and demons bring their own stools. Despite bizarre rules, a long line forms daily. Customers praise the owner as a genius and warn others never to kidnap the owner or dine and dash.

## Thématique culinaire
The third season continues the restaurant concept with even more bizarre rules and customers. The chef's reputation grows as a genius, and the culinary experience in this fantasy world becomes increasingly central to the narrative.

## Éléments fantastiques
Season 3 expands the fantasy world further with continued appearances of elves, dwarves, orcs, dragons, and demons. The restaurant is a safe haven in a chaotic world.

## Pertinence pour le projet
Very High — The ongoing expansion of the restaurant concept across three seasons provides a deep well of inspiration for a fantasy restaurant game with progressive complexity.
