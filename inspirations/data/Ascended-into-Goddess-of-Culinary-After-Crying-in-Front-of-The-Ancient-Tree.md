## Résumé
Pepper was an assistant chef at Crimson Dragon Tavern. She was at the lowest in her life when a power of a goddess bestowed upon her.

## Thématique culinaire
Pepper is an assistant chef whose culinary career is central to her identity. The story follows her journey from the lowest position in a tavern to being bestowed with divine culinary power by a goddess.

## Éléments fantastiques
Fantasy elements include a goddess bestowing power, the Crimson Dragon Tavern setting, and the concept of ascending to become a 'Goddess of Culinary.' The divine empowerment arc provides a classic fantasy progression.
