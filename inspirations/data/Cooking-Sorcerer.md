## Résumé
Being a major foodie, tattoo artist Yoojung Seo always felt sorry for the male lead of her favorite webnovel. Unable to taste due to being poisoned by his mother, Felix never saw food as anything more than a hindrance. Now transmigrated into the novel as the sorcerer Sylvia Arbor, Yoojung finally has the chance to introduce Felix to the wonderful world of delicious cuisine!

## Thématique culinaire
