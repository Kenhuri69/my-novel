## Résumé
At 29, Noura — a workaholic at a top gaming company — died in a car accident. She woke up in a parallel world, determined to pursue her long-neglected passion for cooking. With zero culinary knowledge of this world, Noura must adapt while honing her cooking abilities and building a small eatery from scratch.
