## Résumé
Qin Xiaoguo, a successful chef from the year 3024, transmigrates to 42 BC. She wakes in a famine-stricken land and discovers a hungry boy who calls her mother. Her only advantage is a spatial ring that grants access to food from her original era.

## Thématique culinaire
Cooking is central — the protagonist is a chef from the future who uses her culinary skills and a spatial ring with food supplies to survive in ancient China.

## Éléments fantastiques
Transmigration to ancient China with a spatial ring containing future food and belongings. Fantasy elements include the spatial ring and ancient Chinese setting.
