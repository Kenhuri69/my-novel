## Résumé
Min Yoongi, the icy CEO of South Korea's culinary empire, is renowned for his unwavering pursuit of perfection. His world revolves around crafting the ultimate gastronomic experience, and finding the ideal chef for his flagship restaurant is his sole focus. Into this meticulously constructed life steps Park Jimin, a whirlwind of energy and talent who unknowingly becomes the cornerstone of Yoongi's world. Their connection deepens amidst the sizzling kitchen atmosphere, but fate has a cruel twist in store. Yoongi's father, a formidable patriarch, has already plotted his son's future, a future that doesn't include Jimin. When the truth of this arranged marriage shatters their idyllic world, Jimin is faced with a heart-wrenching decision: surrender to fate or fight for the love that defines them.

## Thématique culinaire
The culinary empire and restaurant setting provide the backdrop for the romance. Yoongi's role as a culinary CEO and the 'sizzling kitchen atmosphere' place cooking at the center of the story's emotional and professional conflicts.

## Éléments fantastiques
Primarily a contemporary romance with no fantasy elements. The culinary empire setting provides a high-stakes professional backdrop but lacks supernatural or fantastical elements.

## Pertinence pour le projet
Low-Medium — Strong culinary setting (culinary empire, restaurant) but primarily a romance with no fantasy elements. The culinary empire concept could inspire game settings but the lack of fantasy limits its direct applicability.
