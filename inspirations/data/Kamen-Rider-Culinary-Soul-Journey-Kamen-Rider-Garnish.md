## Résumé
In a world where flavor is tied to the human soul, a cold corporate entity known as the Malnutrition Empire has begun a 'Great Cleansing.' Four estranged graduates of the Apex Culinary Academy pilot high-tech Ridercarts and transform via the Culin-Driver to battle the Empire's monsters and revive the community's 'Culinary Soul.'

## Thématique culinaire
Cooking and culinary skills are tied directly to power in this world. The Culinary Academy trains warriors who use cooking-based transformations to fight. The 'Culin-Driver' transformation device is explicitly culinary-themed.

## Éléments fantastiques
Kamen Rider AU with a culinary twist. The Culin-Driver transformation system, Ridercarts, and the Malnutrition Empire as antagonists provide a superhero/fantasy framework around the cooking theme.

## Pertinence pour le projet
High — The concept of cooking-based transformation and power (Culin-Driver) is directly inspired for game mechanics. Could inspire a system where cooking unlocks combat abilities or transformations.
