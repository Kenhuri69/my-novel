## Résumé
When single mother and baker Autumn Milford is hired to cater a high-end party in the Berkshires, she discovers the 'special ingredient' she is required to use isn't just the quirk of an eccentric millionaire. Corbin Westbrook hosts a once-a-decade gathering of the New England Knowing, tasked with disposing of a Fallen creature. Their paths cross when Autumn is hired as the baker, and she learns creatures of the night are real—and enjoying her cinnamon rolls.

## Thématique culinaire
Baking and pastry-making are central to the story. Autumn's skills as a baker are the key to her survival and success. The story explores the connection between food and the supernatural world, with baked goods playing a role in the paranormal narrative.

## Éléments fantastiques
Paranormal/fantasy elements including vampires, creatures of the night, and a secret society (the New England Knowing). The supernatural world intersects with the culinary world through the 'special ingredient' requirement.

## Pertinence pour le projet
Medium — Combines baking/pastry with paranormal fantasy elements. The concept of supernatural creatures enjoying human food could inspire game interactions between fantastical beings and culinary offerings.
