## Résumé
When Jiang Yanyi, heiress to a traditional hotpot family, opened her eyes, she found herself transmigrated into an ancient romance novel as the villainous supporting character. After framing the female lead, she was exiled to a border military camp, where she was treated as mere entertainment for the soldiers. She picks up a cooking ladle, uses modern culinary knowledge to make instant noodles, cola, and hotpot, and catches the eye of the ruthless King of Liaonan, Feng Shuo, who declares her his wife.

## Thématique culinaire
Jiang Yanyi uses her modern culinary knowledge to create dishes like instant noodles, cola, and hotpot in an ancient setting. Cooking becomes her tool for survival and influence, as she uses food to win over the military camp and eventually the king.

## Éléments fantastiques
Transmigration into an ancient Chinese romance novel as a villainess. The isekai premise includes a military camp setting, ancient civilizations, and political intrigue. The cooking ladle serves as both a practical tool and a symbol of her modern knowledge.

## Pertinence pour le projet
Very High — Transmigration + cooking in a historical fantasy setting. The concept of using modern culinary knowledge to survive in a fantasy world is directly applicable to game design, especially for a cooking-based survival or kingdom-building mechanic.
