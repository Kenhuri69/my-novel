## Résumé
The day Ethan Bennett loses his family's five-million-yuan redevelopment payout to his older sister is the day he finally understands a brutal truth: no matter how much he gives, he will always come last. He then walks away, flies to Sydney, and rebuilds his life from dishwashing to building a dining empire, while the family that abandoned him may soon learn what they threw away.

## Thématique culinaire
The story follows Ethan's journey from dishwasher to building a dining empire. Cooking and the restaurant industry are the vehicle for his rise from rags to riches. The culinary career progression is central to the narrative.

## Éléments fantastiques
Primarily a contemporary romance/drama with no fantasy elements. The 'dining empire' concept provides a business/career fantasy rather than a supernatural one.

## Pertinence pour le projet
Medium — The rags-to-riches culinary career arc provides inspiration for game progression mechanics around building a restaurant empire. The 'dishwasher to dining empire' trajectory is a compelling game narrative framework.
