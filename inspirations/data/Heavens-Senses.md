## Résumé
Hikari and her twin brother Soma loved to cook from a young age. Hikari was given a gift — heightened senses (sight, scent, taste, hearing, touch) — making her 'Heaven's Senses.' She walks with her brother to become the greatest chefs, discovering secrets and adventures along the way.

## Thématique culinaire
Cooking is central — Hikari is a gifted chef with supernatural sensory abilities that enhance her cooking.

## Éléments fantastiques
