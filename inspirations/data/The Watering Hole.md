## Résumé
A chef, yanked from his kitchen into a world of beastmen and ancient prophecies, finds himself in a fish-out-of-water power struggle with the key to uniting a divided city on his culinary skills...and becoming a mate.

## Thématique culinaire
Cooking is central to the plot — the protagonist uses his culinary skills to unite a divided city and build a thriving restaurant.
