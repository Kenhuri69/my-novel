## Résumé
Kyungho was an ordinary cook before being abducted and forced to fight off a demon invasion in another realm. He returns to a world overrun with monsters and discovers his mother and Earth's guardian deity, Carnis de Lupus, are dying. Kyungho offers to serve revitalizing meals to Carnis in exchange for healing his mother.

## Thématique culinaire
Cooking is central — Kyungho serves revitalizing meals to a guardian deity in exchange for healing his mother. The restaurant/kitchen is the key to both combat and healing.

## Éléments fantastiques
Fantasy world with demons, monsters, a guardian deity, and another realm. Hero summoned to fight demon invasion.

## Pertinence pour le projet
