## Résumé
Takumi Morimoto, an overworked chef, dies saving a starving mouse — only to learn the creature was a god in disguise. Rewarded for his kindness, he is reborn as a brown mouse in a cozy forest world with a chef's hat-shaped marking and a 'Tiny Gourmet System.'

## Thématique culinaire
The protagonist is a chef reborn with a 'Tiny Gourmet System' — a cooking-themed game system in a fantasy world. Chef skills and culinary abilities are central.

## Éléments fantastiques
Isekai reincarnation as a mouse in a fantasy forest world. LitRPG elements with a 'Tiny Gourmet System.' Gods, magic, and fantasy creatures.
