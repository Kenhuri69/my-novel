                                  In order to help food culture flourish, culinary delicacies manifest as "Food Souls" and appear in the mortal realm.
                                  
                                  Food Souls uphold the lifelong mission of transmitting food culture.
                                  Should the delicacies' traditions die out in the mortal realm, so would the life of the Food Souls themselves.
                                  
                                  The Food God, Yi Zhi, thus signed a "contract" with these Food Souls.
                                  In order to maintain the unceasing transmission of culinary traditions, the names of the Food Souls were recorded in a revered book known as "The Tale of Food", and "Kong Sang" was built as a protective haven.
                                  
                                  Years have passed since, and "Kong Sang" has become the most famous gourmet paradise of the Three Realms.
                                  
                                  "The Tale of Food" is not merely proof of the contracts with the Food Souls, it is also a comprehensive compilation of the cooking methods and cultural history of Chinese gourmet cuisine.
                                  
                                  And now, "The Tale of Food" has been inherited by two children, the "Young Masters" who are the heir of the food God...
                                  
                                  Every day, with the protection of the Food Souls who watched over them, they grew up and eventually became an exemplary "Food God's Representatives".
                                  
                                  However, times have changed, and malevolent spirits have become rampant.
                                  The enemies of the Food God harbour an overwhelming conspiracy, plotting to carry out their grudges of ages past, and stands in their way.
                                  
                                  As the Food God's Representatives, they shoulder the grave responsibility of protecting the Food Souls, and set forth towards an unknown world...
