## Résumé
Shadow Milk is isekai-ed into a fairytale world where the glass slipper is replaced by the Soul Jam, a pastry-like magical item. A Cookie Run Kingdom x Cinderella crossover.

## Thématique culinaire
The Soul Jam is a pastry-like magical item central to the plot. Cookie Run Kingdom is a dessert-themed world.

## Éléments fantastiques
Isekai into a fairytale world with magic, spells, and Cookie Run Kingdom characters (Shadow Milk Cookie, Pure Vanilla Cookie).
