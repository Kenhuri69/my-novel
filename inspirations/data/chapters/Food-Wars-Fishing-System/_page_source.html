Waking up in the World of Food Wars-Yuuki gains a unique edge: The Fishing System. This mysterious system grants him fishing bait of varying qualities-Depending on the bait used, Yuuki can reel in incredible rewards pulled from-countless different Worlds. With Small Bait-Yuuki landed a Legendary set of Seven-Star Knives. Large Bait yielded powerful Flame Abilities. And the Super Bait? It allowed him to hook the very Essence of Culinary Respect from the Toriko Universe-Food Honor. Beyond that-special "Growth Bait" becomes available as Yuuki himself levels up and improves his skills. Armed with his Fishing System-Yuuki casts his line into Totsuki Academy, ready to start his overpowered Culinary journey and reel in victory after victory.
