In the Chaos City of Nolan Continent, there's a peculiar restaurant. 

Here, elves have to share tables with dwarves, orcs are strictly forbidden from making noise, dragons can only sit around the small square in front of the restaurant, and demons even need to bring their own special stools... 

Yet, despite these bizarre rules, a long line forms outside the restaurant every day. 

Elves eat skewers without regard for etiquette, dragons huddle around a hotpot with ladles in hand, and demons munch on adorable dumplings... 

"The food here is unlike anything else on the continent! This owner is a genius!" one customer commented, then secretly glanced towards the entrance: "And, whatever you do, don't even think about kidnapping the owner or trying to dine and dash, or you'll die a horrible death." 

 "Eat and pay, or I'll kill you all." a cute little girl said in a sweet, childish voice, her gaze falling on a nearby dragon, causing the five or six meter tall dragon to shiver.

The Cannon Fodder in Quick Transmigration Refuses the Usual Path

In every world, she is supposed to be cannon fodder a side character meant to suffer. But she refuses to accept that fate.

As a palace maid in the Qing dynasty, she avoids dangerous schemes and quietly watches the drama. As an abused stepdaughter in the 1970s, she fights back instead of enduring it. As a poor farmer's wife in ancient times, she doesn't cling to a useless husband. In a dramatic CEO romance, she refuses to be a heartbroken ex-girlfriend.

Whether she is in ancient times, a cultivation world, or a retro countryside setting, Shen Anning always chooses herself. She won't be used, sacrificed, or controlled.

There is no fixed love interest.

Only Shen Anning changing her destiny in every world she enters.

My Wife Hear My Thoughts While I Watch The Drama
I, Pei Cijing, transmigrated into the body of the second young master of a marquis's mansion, and all I want to do is lie back and watch the show.

Who would have thought that on the eve of my wedding, my elder brother, the young master, would end up sleeping with my fiancée!

I was just watching the drama unfold, and the victim turned out to be me?

Just when I thought I was about to become the laughingstock of the entire capital, the eldest daughter of the Shen family, who was supposed to become my sister-in-law, actually took the initiative to propose:

"How about I marry you instead?"

I remained outwardly calm, but inwardly I was overjoyed: This girl has good taste!

But it wasn't until one day after our marriage that I realized something was wrong. My inner monologue was pounding: "My wife looks so beautiful today, I want to kiss her..."

She suddenly turned her head, her eyes sparkling: "Granted."

Can she hear my thoughts?!

Now look what's happened! I've tasted all the gossip in the world, but she only tastes my stupidity.
