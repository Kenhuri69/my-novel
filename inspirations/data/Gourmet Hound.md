## Résumé
Lucy possesses an uncanny ability to perceive flavors and aromas. When her cherished restaurant replaces its kitchen crew, she cannot identify the chef responsible for the dishes she has long adored. A chance meeting with two former chefs inspires her to track down their old colleagues.

## Thématique culinaire
