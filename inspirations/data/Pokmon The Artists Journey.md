## Résumé
With a sketchbook in her bag and Squirtle at her side, Olivia begins her journey in Kanto with a dream unlike any other: to become the best Pokémon artist she can be while growing as a trainer. She travels from Kanto to Paldea through art, cooking, and bonds with Pokémon.
