## Résumé
Dragons and humans have always been at odds. A young cook befriends a dragoness through an act of selflessness, and a beautiful friendship is born. Their bond will test the boundaries of everything they stand for, steering the Shattered Isle onto a path no one could have predicted.

## Thématique culinaire
Cooking is central — the protagonist is a young cook who befriends a dragoness through cooking.

## Éléments fantastiques
Fantasy world with dragons, dragon kingdoms, and a Shattered Isle setting.
