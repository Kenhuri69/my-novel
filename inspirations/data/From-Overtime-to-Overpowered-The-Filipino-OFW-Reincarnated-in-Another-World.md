## Résumé
Filipino OFW Miguel Santos is reincarnated in another world after a mysterious accident at the airport. A goddess reveals he wasn't supposed to be summoned — the legendary Hero was meant to come from Japan. Armed with his life experience and a balikbayan box, Miguel's cooking brings enemies to the same table and his practical thinking solves impossible problems.

## Thématique culinaire
Cooking is central — Miguel's legendary cooking brings enemies together and his cooking skills awaken as powerful abilities.

## Éléments fantastiques
Isekai with magical kingdoms, dragons, demons, elves, ancient magic, and a divine mistake that reincarnated an OFW instead of a Japanese hero.
