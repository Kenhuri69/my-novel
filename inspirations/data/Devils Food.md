## Résumé
Marcy sells her soul to the demon Mephistopheles for a taco. To reclaim it, she must fulfill his request: 'Teach him how to cook!' A unique blend of demonic fantasy and culinary themes.

## Thématique culinaire
The protagonist must teach the demon Mephistopheles how to cook to reclaim her soul. Strong culinary themes centered on cooking lessons for a supernatural patron.
