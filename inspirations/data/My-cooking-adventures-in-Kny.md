## Résumé
Given a second chance, y/n, lives her life to the fullest! She wants to be able to cook in comfort in her new life but can't do that until the demons are defeated! However, she doesn't plan on just killing all the demons, she wants to save them as well! (Or at least the ones that deserve to be saved.) What will this intelligent reincarnation do?

## Thématique culinaire
Cooking is a central theme as the protagonist uses her culinary skills in a world of demon slayers. The story combines food preparation with the demon-slaying genre, offering a unique blend of culinary arts and supernatural action.

## Éléments fantastiques
Reincarnation into the Demon Slayer universe with a second chance at life. The protagonist possesses knowledge of both her past life and the world she's entered, allowing her to navigate the dangers of the demon world while pursuing her culinary dreams.
