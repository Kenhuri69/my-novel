## Résumé
Waking up in the World of Food Wars-Yuuki gains a unique edge: The Fishing System. This mysterious system grants him fishing bait of varying qualities. Depending on the bait used, Yuuki can reel in incredible rewards pulled from countless different Worlds.

## Thématique culinaire
Set in the Food Wars universe, cooking is a core element. The Fishing System adds a unique twist by allowing the protagonist to catch ingredients from different worlds, creating a culinary adventure with fantastical ingredients.

## Éléments fantastiques
The Fishing System is a game-like mechanic that allows the protagonist to catch items from different worlds. This isekai-style system provides fantasy elements alongside the culinary setting of Food Wars.

## Pertinence pour le projet
High — Combines the Food Wars culinary world with an isekai-style fishing system. The concept of catching ingredients from other worlds could inspire game mechanics for ingredient gathering or a fishing-based cooking system.
