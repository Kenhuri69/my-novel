## Résumé
Reborn as a lady into a strange family. How will our protagonist achieve her life's dream of starting a bakery when she has to worry about living until 14 first? The story follows Corvalia, who has been taken over by someone else after losing her life in the modern world. She vows to start a bakery in the land of Audora, saying 'I'll do it this time! No matter what!'

## Thématique culinaire
Baking is the protagonist's life dream and central activity. The story follows Corvalia's journey to start a bakery in the fantasy land of Audora. The bakery serves as the primary vehicle for the protagonist's goals and dreams.

## Éléments fantastiques
Reincarnation into a fantasy world called Audora. The protagonist is reborn as a lady in a strange family and must navigate a fantasy setting to achieve her dream of opening a bakery.

## Pertinence pour le projet
High — The bakery-in-a-fantasy-world concept provides direct inspiration for game mechanics around bakery management, recipe discovery, and fantasy world-building.
