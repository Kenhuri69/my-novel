## Résumé
A gambling addict who betrayed his family receives a second chance in another world, seeking redemption. The story features the deity Gula, linking to culinary/gluttony themes, and includes isekai elements.

## Thématique culinaire
Gluttony/deity Gula is a central theme — culinary excess and the god of food/gluttony. The protagonist seeks redemption through a second chance.

## Éléments fantastiques
Isekai into another world with fantasy elements. Deity Gula (gluttony) as a key figure. Gamelit/harem elements.
