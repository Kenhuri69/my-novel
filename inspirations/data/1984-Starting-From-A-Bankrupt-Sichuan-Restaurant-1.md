## Résumé
Food blogger Zhou Yan, reborn in 1984, takes over a bankrupt Sichuan restaurant. In this era of scarcity, he offers diners a taste of high-quality cuisine! Cross-legged beef, husband and wife lung slices, mapo tofu, dongpo pork knuckle, diving rabbit... Stir-fry, sauté, roast, steam, deep-fry, boil, braise, smoke... Each dish has its own unique style, each dish offers a unique flavor! From then on, a small town in southern Sichuan gained a renowned small restaurant.

## Thématique culinaire
Sichuan cuisine is the heart of the story. The protagonist uses his modern food blogger knowledge and culinary skills to revive a bankrupt restaurant, creating authentic and high-quality Sichuan dishes. The detailed descriptions of cooking techniques (stir-fry, sauté, roast, steam, deep-fry, boil, braise, smoke) provide rich culinary content.

## Éléments fantastiques
Primarily a historical fiction with limited fantasy elements. The rebirth/reincarnation premise provides a time-travel element, but the setting is grounded in 1984 China. The culinary focus is more realistic than fantastical.

## Pertinence pour le projet
Medium — Strong culinary focus on Sichuan cuisine with a rebirth premise. The detailed cooking techniques and the journey from a bankrupt restaurant to a renowned one could inspire game mechanics around restaurant management and recipe discovery.
