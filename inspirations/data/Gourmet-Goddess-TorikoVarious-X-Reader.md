## Résumé
A young girl is born with an insurmountable gift to shape the culinary and gourmet world. She becomes a Gourmet Goddess — a Gourmet Hunter, Chef, Food-Lover, and Adventurer. Follow her journey through the Gourmet Age as she catches the eyes of the Four Heavenly Kings and others who vie for her heart or seek to take her by force. She is no pushover when fighting for herself and her friends.

## Thématique culinaire
The protagonist is a Gourmet Goddess who shapes the culinary world. Cooking, gourmet hunting, and food adventures are the core of the story. The Gourmet Age setting provides a rich backdrop for culinary exploration.

## Éléments fantastiques
Set in the Toriko universe with Gourmet Hunters, powerful beasts, and a world where food has incredible properties. The Gourmet Goddess concept adds a mythological/fantasy layer to the culinary setting.
