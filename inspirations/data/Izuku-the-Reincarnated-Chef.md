## Résumé
Due to Katsuki's wounded Ego, Izuku is blasted away as a 'Thank you' for saving the blond's life from the Sludge Villain and ends in a year-long coma. During that year, Izuku is transported to another world where he lives a second life as Zaus, a Chef in a world where cooking and Ingredients rule supreme. He rises to become the 'King of Chefs,' is then brainwashed into a Villain, and falls from grace. Waking from his coma with memories from both lives, Izuku has an epiphany: 'IF he can't be a Hero, he will just be a Chef once again!' However, he is still Midoriya Izuku—the greatest magnet for troubles—so his 'Unnatural' Skills soon put him on the radar of both Heroes and Villains. His cry: 'For God's sake! I just want to manage my Restaurant! Leave me alone!'

## Thématique culinaire
Cooking is the central profession and passion of the protagonist. In the other world, Izuku becomes Zaus, the King of Chefs, and culinary skill is the primary measure of power and status. The story explores the intersection of cooking ability and combat.

## Éléments fantastiques
Isekai/reincarnation into the Toriko universe where cooking ability equals power. The 'King of Chefs' concept and the culinary-based power system provide strong fantasy elements. The protagonist also retains memories of My Hero Academia.

## Pertinence pour le projet
Very High — Combines isekai transmigration with a cooking-centric power system. The concept of 'culinary power' where cooking skill translates to combat ability is directly inspired for game mechanics.
