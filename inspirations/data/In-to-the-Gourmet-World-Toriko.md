## Résumé
Mari is in for food, adventure, and maybe even romance along the way. How will she survive in this new world and who will win her heart? A regular young adult who loved anime, especially Toriko, was transported there by a storm.

## Thématique culinaire
Set in the Gourmet World of Toriko, cooking and gourmet ingredients are central to the world-building. The protagonist uses her culinary knowledge to navigate this world full of exotic and powerful ingredients.

## Éléments fantastiques
Transported to the Toriko universe, a world where gourmet ingredients have incredible powers and the Gourmet Hunters explore dangerous territories to find them. The setting is deeply fantastical with powerful beasts and ancient culinary traditions.

## Pertinence pour le projet
High — Set in the Toriko universe, a world entirely built around gourmet food and culinary adventure. The combination of fantasy elements with a food-centric world provides rich inspiration for game design.
