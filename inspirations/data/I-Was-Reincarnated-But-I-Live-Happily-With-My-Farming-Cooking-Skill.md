## Résumé
Aletta Krea is reincarnated as a baby with memories of her previous life as a chef. She was born into a ducal house but was unlucky — her mother passed away after birth and she was kidnapped. She was adopted by the Krea family, who have been working in a restaurant for generations but now work as farmers. She uses her cooking and farming skills to help them make the restaurant a success.

## Thématique culinaire
Cooking and farming are central to the plot — the protagonist uses her culinary skills to revitalize a family restaurant.
