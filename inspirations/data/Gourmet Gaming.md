## Résumé
Kang Minhyuk suffers from a rare disorder that makes it impossible for him to stop eating. His doctor recommends a unique treatment — Athenae, a VR game where one can eat as much as they want without consequence. Inside Athenae, all food tastes real, but earning it is no cakewalk. In order to live, Minhyuk must go on the ultimate fantasy gourmet adventure!

## Thématique culinaire
Cooking and food are central — the VR game world features real-tasting food that must be earned through challenges.
