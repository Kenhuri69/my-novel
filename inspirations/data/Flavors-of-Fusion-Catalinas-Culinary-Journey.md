## Résumé
Catalina Rodriguez, a talented Latina chef from the United States, embarks on a culinary adventure. With expertise in fusion cooking, baking, and Latin and Mexican cuisine, her path intertwines with Totsuki Culinary Academy and its characters from the anime Food Wars.

## Thématique culinaire
Fusion cooking, baking, and Latin/Mexican cuisine are the protagonist's specialties. The story is set in the Food Wars universe with Totsuki Culinary Academy as the main setting. The fusion of different culinary traditions is a key theme.

## Éléments fantastiques
Set in the Food Wars universe with its culinary school setting and super-powered cooking abilities. The anime-inspired world provides a fantastical backdrop for the culinary adventure.
