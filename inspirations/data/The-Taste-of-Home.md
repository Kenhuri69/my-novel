## Résumé
Miguel, a chupacabra, finds his life changing after protecting a human and getting a chef job. As he falls for vocal cook Alejandro, he wonders, 'But what human would ever love a chupacabra?'

## Thématique culinaire
Cooking is Miguel's new profession and path to a new life. As a chef, he finds purpose and connection. The workplace setting of a kitchen provides the primary setting for both the culinary and romantic elements of the story.

## Éléments fantastiques
Paranormal/fantasy elements including a chupacabra protagonist and a supernatural workplace romance. The chupacabra as a chef in a human kitchen creates an interesting fantasy-culinary intersection.

## Pertinence pour le projet
Medium — The paranormal chef concept (chupacabra working in a kitchen) provides a unique culinary-fantasy intersection. The workplace romance in a kitchen setting could inspire game scenarios around cooking and relationships.
