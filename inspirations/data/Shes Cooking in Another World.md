## Résumé
Master of the Cooking Arts, Lily Zen, is transported to another world. She is astounded at the lack of culinary development and decides to change this, rising to become a Legendary figure. This may be a world of magic but there should always be delicious food!

## Thématique culinaire
Cooking is central — Lily Zen is a master chef who brings culinary development to another world.
