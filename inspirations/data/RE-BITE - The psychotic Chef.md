## Résumé
A horror comic following married gay protagonists who kill and devour victims. Louise races to become a professional chef and win a cooking tournament. Features bi-monthly cooking battle seasons and high-level cooking classes.

## Thématique culinaire
Professional chef aspirations, cooking tournaments, high-level cooking classes, and group-based cooking battles.

## Éléments fantastiques
Minimal fantasy elements. Psychological horror with a culinary setting.
