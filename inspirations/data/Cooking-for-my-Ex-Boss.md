## Résumé
When Audrey, the smart, beautiful and kind-hearted woman decided to quit her job to pursue her dream as a chef, she did not expect to meet her ex-boss again, Ethan Loyd. The story follows their incompatible souls being forced together, with Audrey discovering his hidden secrets.

## Thématique culinaire
Audrey's journey as a chef is central to the plot. She quits her job to pursue her dream of cooking, and the culinary world serves as the backdrop for the romance and drama. The story ranks #1 in Chef, Cooking, and Completed on Wattpad.

## Éléments fantastiques
Primarily a contemporary romance with limited fantasy elements. The culinary world provides the setting but there are no supernatural or fantastical elements.

## Pertinence pour le projet
Medium — Strong culinary focus but lacks fantasy elements. Useful for understanding how cooking is portrayed in a professional setting and the emotional connection between characters and food.
