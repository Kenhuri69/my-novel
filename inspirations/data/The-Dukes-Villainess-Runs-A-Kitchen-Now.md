## Résumé
A modern-day culinary student dies from overwork and wakes up inside her favorite fantasy novel — except she isn't the heroine. She transmigrates into Lady Seraphina Valecrest, the infamous villainess destined to ruin the heroine's life, poison nobles, and eventually be executed publicly. But she remembers every chapter and wants nothing to do with the tragic ending. Instead of fighting over princes and power, she pursues cooking. However, changing fate isn't easy when the cold crown prince suspects her, the original heroine keeps appearing, nobles refuse to believe a 'villainess' can change, and political conspiracies unfold around her restaurant. Her food comforts broken people, reveals hidden memories, and softens hearts.

## Thématique culinaire
Cooking is the protagonist's chosen path to redemption and survival. She opens a restaurant in a fantasy kingdom, using her culinary skills to change perceptions and influence political outcomes. Food becomes her weapon against the tragic fate that awaits the villainess.

## Éléments fantastiques
Transmigration into a fantasy novel as a villainess. The setting includes magical kingdoms, political intrigue, crown princes, and noble conspiracies. The restaurant exists within this fantasy political landscape.

## Pertinence pour le projet
Very High — Transmigration + cooking in a fantasy kingdom setting. The concept of using a restaurant as a tool for political influence and redemption in a fantasy world is directly applicable to game design, especially for kingdom-building or social influence mechanics.
