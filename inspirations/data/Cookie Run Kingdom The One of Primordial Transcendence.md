## Résumé
The MC gets reincarnated into Cookie Run: Kingdom in the body of a Cookie — specifically, the Cookie OC they created themselves. Dropped into this world without warning, their phone arrives, now capable of summoning owned Cookies who all know the MC is their Baker. The story follows the MC alongside Gingerbrave's team as they navigate a magical world of desserts, defeat looming darkness, and discover the powers of the Cookie Vessel.

## Thématique culinaire
Cooking/baking is central — the MC is a Baker in the Cookie Run Kingdom, a world of desserts where cookies have jam-blood and flavor-based abilities.
