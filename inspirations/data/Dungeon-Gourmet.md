## Résumé
In a world full of dungeons, monsters, magic, and non-humans, Marron Glace is a surprisingly strong shield bearer whose past is as mysterious as his constantly covered face. He can only stomach food made from monsters.

## Thématique culinaire
Cooking is central — Marron can only eat food made from monsters, making his appetite a key plot element.

## Éléments fantastiques
Fantasy world with dungeons, monsters, magic, and non-human races. Strong world-building.

## Pertinence pour le projet
High — Fantasy dungeon-crawling with a unique culinary twist (eating monster food).
